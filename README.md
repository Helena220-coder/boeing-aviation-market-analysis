Boeing Aviation Market Analysis
Project Overview

This project analyzes airline financial and operational data from 2010–2026 to understand trends in the commercial aviation industry and their potential implications for Boeing.

The analysis focuses on airline revenue, passenger volume, operating margins, load factors, fleet size, regional performance, and the impact of COVID-19 on the aviation industry.

Business Questions
Which airlines generated the highest revenue?
Which airlines carried the most passengers?
How has airline revenue changed from 2010–2026?
How did COVID-19 affect the airline industry?
Which regions generated the most revenue?
Which regions have the largest airline fleets?
Which airlines have the highest operating margins?
Does fleet size relate to airline revenue?
Does load factor affect airline profitability?
Which business models perform better?

Key Findings
North America generated the highest airline revenue among the regions analyzed.
Delta Air Lines, American Airlines, United Airlines, Lufthansa Airlines, and Air France-KLM were among the highest-revenue airlines in the analysis.
Airline revenue and passenger activity declined significantly during the COVID-19 period.
The industry began recovering after 2020, with revenue and passenger activity increasing in subsequent years.
Boeing Business Implications

The analysis provides a market-level view of commercial aviation that can help identify important trends for Boeing.

Strong airline revenue, passenger demand, and regional performance can indicate markets that are important for the commercial aviation industry. The analysis also shows how major disruptions such as COVID-19 can affect airline financial performance and passenger demand.

These findings provide context for understanding airline market conditions and potential aircraft demand.
